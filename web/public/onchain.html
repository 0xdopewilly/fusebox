Fusebox wires an AI agent's wallet through small logic circuits taped out on TapeOut. A payment reaches the payee only if every fuse holds. This copy of Fusebox has no server. Its bytes live on X Layer in the container of circuit #15, TapeKit serves them, and your browser reads every number below straight from the chain.

[02] Try to scam it

Scam [Atlas].

Atlas is a live Fusebox wallet on X Layer with four fuses. Pick a trick an invoice scammer would use, and your browser asks Atlas's check() what its fuses would do. It's a read-only call: no wallet, no gas, nothing moves.

Each fuse is a NAND circuit on TapeOut's OrderGuard processor. The wallet runs them all in one call.
