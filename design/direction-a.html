Fuses for your agent's wallet.

Your AI agent can only spend through circuits you pick: a daily cap, a list of payees, a breaker, a two-of-three sign-off. If any of them says no, the money stays put. Whoever designed that circuit is paid for the decision, yes or no.

One payment, four checks.

Every payment your agent tries is put to every fuse on the panel, in order. It goes through only if all of them say yes.

2
1

Your agent asks

It names the payee, the amount and a reason. That is all it controls. The fuses read everything else from the wallet itself.

2

Each fuse decides

A small logic circuit on X Layer answers yes or no. The Breaker is 11 gates; the Daily Cap, the largest, is 108.

3

All yes, money moves

One no and nothing leaves the wallet. The decision and the fuse that stopped it are written on chain for you to read.

4

The designer is paid

Every fuse that decided earns its fee in USDT0, including the ones that said no. Designers keep 90%.

A payment checked by all four fuses costs 0.004500 USDT0 in fees and about 0.000018 OKB in gas.
Tested the long way: the Daily Cap circuit was run against all 16,777,216 possible inputs before it went on chain.
