Circuits that tell your agent no, and get paid for it.

An AI agent's wallet in Fusebox can only pay through fuses: small logic circuits taped out on TapeOut and run on X Layer. Every payment is checked by every fuse. If one says no, nothing moves. Every fuse that checked it pays its designer a fee in USDT0.

  • The agent chooses only payee, amount and reason; fuses read the rest from the wallet
  • Stack up to 8 fuses per wallet, in the order you choose
  • Blocked attempts are recorded on chain, with the fuse that stopped them
  • Designers keep 90% of each fee; the circuit's owner can sell the income

2How a payment is decided

2.1The agent asks

It names the payee, the amount and a reason. That is all it controls; it cannot touch a fuse, a cap or the payee list.

2.2Every fuse checks

The wallet turns the payment into a few bits (amount, spent today, payee saved or not) and asks each circuit for one bit back: yes or no.

2.3All yes, or nothing

One no and the payment stops. A Breaker that says no keeps saying it, until the owner resets it.

2.4The designers are paid

Each fuse that decided earns its fee, the no's included. Stopping a bad payment is the job.

That is the fee for one payment checked by all four fuses in Table 1, plus about 0.000018 OKB of gas on X Layer. Designers keep 90%; Fusebox keeps 10%, and the contract caps its share at 20%.

Fees come from a balance you top up separately, so the agent's spending money and its safety budget never mix.
