The wallet is one wire from your agent to the payee, with the fuses in series on it. Lime is current: it reaches the payee only when every fuse is closed. A blown fuse is a visible break in the line. It's nocturnal and exact, in X Layer's black and white.

Every payment is put to every fuse on the line, in order. The agent can only say who, how much and why; everything a fuse reads comes from the wallet itself.

  1. 21

    The agent asks to pay

    Payee, amount, a one-line reason. That is the agent's whole say. It cannot change a fuse, a cap or a payee.

  2. 2

    Each fuse checks it on X Layer

    Each fuse is a circuit of 11 to 108 logic gates, taped out on TapeOut. It answers yes or no in one call.

  3. 3

    Every yes, or nothing moves

    One no and the payment stops. The wallet records which fuse said no and why, on chain.

  4. 4

    The designers are paid

    Each fuse that decided earns its fee in USDT0, the no's included. A circuit that stops a scam has done its job.
